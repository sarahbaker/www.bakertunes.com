One of the lovely things about today's world is that people can easily come across your work and get in contact.

I had a surprise email out of the blue in July from [Julian Lambert](https://www.julianlambert.org/), a well-known teacher and pianist who has a successful [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/JulianLambert) channel and online teaching site, specialising in the graded exam repertoire. 

He had been asked by a subscriber if he would do a sepcial focus video on my piece, *Shadows*, and was writing to ask my permission to feature it on his channels. It took me about 3 mico-seconds to say yes! 

He also wondered if I would be interested in recording a conversation about the piece to describe the inspiriation and story behind it.

Well, of course I wanted to talk about my music! 

So later that day we had an engaging chat about how I came to compose the piece and the elements I wanted to include in the music to convey the original imaginative concept. 
 
He's done a fantastic job of putting it all together and I'm really grateful to Julian for this opportunity to share my music more widely. 

[Watch Julian's video here.](https://youtu.be/rGaUjhH6Kqk)

---
*Shadows* is the fourth piece in my collection, *Night Time Impressions* - a series of 8 piano pieces that takes the performer and listener on a journey from dusk to dawn through the darkness of the night. I'm delighted that it has been selected for the ABRSM Grade 3 piano syllabuses, 2025-29. A short blog about the piece can be found [here](https://medium.com/@bakertunes/shadows-20dcfb3566b9)
