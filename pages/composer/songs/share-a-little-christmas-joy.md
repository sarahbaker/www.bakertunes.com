Each Christmas I organise a concert for my music students to perform to friends and family. In the last couple of years I have finished the event by teaching the audience a Christmas round.

*Share a little Christmas joy* is the song I composed for my 2024 Christmas concert - it was great fun and made a lovely conclusion to the evening.
